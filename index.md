# Delete Your Account – HopeMove

You can request deletion of your account and associated data at any time.

## How to Delete Your Account
1. Log in to the HopeMove app.
2. Go to **Profile > Account Settings > Delete Account**.
3. Confirm the deletion request.

Once confirmed, your account and all personal data (including symptoms, exercise progress, and profile information) will be permanently deleted from our servers within **30 days**.  

## Additional Information
- Deleted data cannot be recovered.  
- Some logs may be retained for up to 90 days for security and legal compliance.
